FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order size? ▼
Our minimum order is for 50 guests for buffet service, and 30 guests for thali/plate service. For smaller gatherings, please call us to discuss options.
How far in advance should I book? ▼
For weddings, we recommend booking 3–6 months in advance. For other events, 2–4 weeks notice is ideal.
Do you offer tasting sessions? ▼
Yes! We offer complimentary tasting sessions for bookings of 200+ guests.
Is your food completely vegetarian? ▼
Yes, we serve 100% pure vegetarian food only. We also accommodate Jain menus and fasting-friendly menus upon request.
Do you provide crockery and serving staff? ▼
Yes, all our packages include cutlery, serving dishes, chafing dishes, and trained serving staff.
What is your cancellation policy? ▼
Cancellations made 15+ days before the event receive a full refund. Less than 7 days: no refund on the advance amount paid.
